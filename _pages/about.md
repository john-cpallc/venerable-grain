  <div class="prose about-block">
    <p>Venerable Grain is a hobby workshop in a Japandi spirit — Japanese quiet, Scandinavian warmth. Pieces are meant to take daily use, come apart when they need to move, and stay simple: few cuts, joinery you can see. Wood first, often upcycled; metal, earth, fabric, and leather when they earn a place.</p>
